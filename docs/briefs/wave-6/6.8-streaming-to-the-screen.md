# Brief 6.8: streaming to the screen, with a reset on retry

Before you write anything, read these five files in this order: `CLAUDE.md` for the rules and the commands, `COEUS.md` for the plain-words explanation of the state design and the check table that ties each claim to a brief and a test, `ARCHITECTURE.md` for how the code is put together and what earlier waves built, `REPO_MAP.md` for where everything lives, and `docs/NERDGENIE_PLAN.md` for the design you are implementing, especially the section this brief names. Then read this brief. This brief crosses five packages on purpose, because a delta has to travel through all of them or through none; one worker does it on one branch so `main` stays green. Write the tests first.

## What this brief is

The screen's design says a reply streams word by word, coalesced every thirty milliseconds, and the wave 5 checklist judges "streaming visible". Nothing streams today. `internal/provider`'s `WithRetries` and `NewChain` hold every delta of an attempt behind a gate until the attempt succeeds, because a failed attempt's text must never reach the person twice (brief 1.6, finding 12). The wiring therefore passes no `Deltas` at all, `internal/channel` has no path for a delta, and the screen's delta handling is exercised only by its own tests.

## The design

A delta carries text or a reset, never both. The contract's callback changes from `func(delta string)` to `func(delta Delta)`, where `Delta` is `{Text string; Reset bool}`; every provider, the fake model, the retry and the chain, and the loop take the new shape, which is mechanical. The reset is the one new idea: the provider streams every delta of the attempt in progress the moment it arrives, and when that attempt fails and another begins, it sends one reset before the new attempt's first delta, so the person sees the partial reply, then sees it withdrawn, then sees the whole reply exactly once. A model that never fails never sends a reset. `contract.SocketEnvelope.Reset` is already on `main` for the socket's side.

The path, package by package, each with its failing test first:

1. **`internal/contract`.** `Delta`, and `Model.Send(ctx, request, onDelta func(Delta))`. A contract test that the fake model streams and that `CheckModel` sees a reset when the fake is told to fail once mid-stream.
2. **`internal/provider`.** The gate goes; `WithRetries` and `NewChain` pass deltas through and send `Delta{Reset: true}` when an attempt that has streamed text is given up on and another begins. Tests: a model that streams "Hello " then rate-limits and then streams "Hello world" gives the caller `Hello `, a reset, `Hello world`, and `reply.Text == "Hello world"`; the chain does the same across two models. The three real providers and the cli one pass `Delta{Text: ...}`.
3. **`internal/loop`.** `Options.Deltas func(Delta)` passed straight to `Send`; nothing else changes.
4. **`internal/channel`.** The stream gets `SendDelta(ctx, delta Delta)`: every delta goes through the vault's redactor with a held-back tail of `len(contract.RedactedMarker)` runes, so a secret cannot straddle two deltas, and the tail is flushed when the reply is sent; deltas are coalesced no finer than thirty milliseconds on the clock; a reset is sent as a delta envelope with `Reset: true` and clears the held tail; every attached screen gets them in order on the same stream the reply rides. Tests: order, coalescing on the fake clock, the split secret blacked out, the reset.
5. **`internal/tui`.** A delta with `Reset` clears the reply bubble being drawn; the tests it already has cover the rest.
6. **`cmd/nerdgenie`.** The wiring fills `Deltas` from the channel's `SendDelta` and removes the paragraph saying deltas are not asked for. Functional test with a fake model that streams, fails once mid-stream, and streams again: the screen sees the first partial text, a reset, then the whole reply once; and a secret split across two deltas arrives blacked out.
