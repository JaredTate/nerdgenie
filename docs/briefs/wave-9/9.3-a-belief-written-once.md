## Part one: a belief is written once

**What is wrong.** The record refuses a failure whose text repeats a held one (`ErrFailureAlreadyWritten`, four words in five shared, `sameness.go`), but not one whose cause does. The sky task wrote four failures with the one cause "screenshots stay byte-frozen while the page reports 60 fps" in four wordings, and both its rethinks named it again as a cause, because the rethink asks for causes not on the record and nothing checks the answer.

**What to build.** In `internal/record`, a failure whose cause shares four words in five with a held failure's cause is refused with `ErrFailureAlreadyWritten`, and the refusal names the failure that already says it, as the text rule does. In `internal/loop/rethink.go`, after the answer is read, each of the two causes is held against every failure's cause on the record with the same rule; when both are already held, the question is asked once more with one line in front of it, `F11, F12 and F13 already say this; name two causes the record does not hold`, and the second answer is taken as it stands, whatever it says. One send-back per rethink.

## Part two: a pick-up starts with a rethink

**What is wrong.** A task picked up after a stop, by the person's word or by the job, opens on the orientation and the record, so the first thing the model reads is the failure list that caused the stop, and it goes straight back to it. The sky task did that three times.

**What to build.** A task picked up (`Task.ResumeID` set) whose record holds at least one failure opens with a rethink, through `rethinkIfAnswered` as it stands, before the first working call: the record and the newest result in front of the model, the five questions, the answer into the record, and the fresh window on the answer with "Do the Next line now." A picked-up task with no failure on its record opens as now.

## Part three: the harness takes the photograph

**What is wrong.** A done line that says "seen and photographed" is proved today only by the model's own picture and its own judgment of it. The sky task spent hours steering the aircraft for a distinct photograph of each element, then doubting the photograph.

**What to build.** In the done check, beside the file and command checks, a done line whose text holds the word "photograph" or "screenshot" is a line the harness proves itself: it takes the picture through the registry's `browser_screenshot` tool, the way `syntax.go` runs the shell tool, and first through `browser_resize` at each width the line names in the shape "at 1440" or "1440 wide", at most two widths, in the line's order; a line naming no width is photographed at the page's current size. Brief 9.1 makes the screenshot tool's text carry the line `the page drew N frames in a quarter of a second`; read that line, and pin the done line to the picture's result when N is above zero. A picture whose page drew no frames proves nothing, and the refusal says `the harness photographed the page for this line, but the page drew no frames, so the picture proves nothing; make the page draw, then close`. When no browser tool is wired, the line is left to the model's proof as now. Test the parsing against the exact sentence; the fake worker is not yours, so drive the done check with `testkit.NewScriptedTool` standing in for `browser_screenshot` and `browser_resize`.
